## RECOMMENDED LEARNING PATH

1. **Level 1 (Foundations)** — Money, trading vs investing, markets, risk-reward, getting started
2. **Level 2 (Market Basics)** — Charts, candlesticks, market deep dives, S/R, trendlines
3. **Level 3 (Intermediate)** — Day trading fundamentals → Technical indicators → Fundamental analysis → Risk management → Psychology → Plan → Strategies → Broker → Mechanics → Costs
4. **Level 4 (Advanced)** — Options, derivatives, portfolio, advanced technical, intermarket
5. **Level 5 (Quantitative)** — Python, data, backtesting, quant strategies, ML, risk metrics, automation

**Optimal path for beginners:** Complete Level 1 and 2 before any live trading. Complete risk-management and entry-timing before increasing size. Complete trading-plan and psychology before going live.
